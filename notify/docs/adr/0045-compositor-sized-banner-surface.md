# Compositor-sized banner surface

Content-sized layer surfaces may extend off-screen even when all content fits their own viewport. Request compositor-assigned width and height with all four anchors, keeping the existing non-exclusive overlay and no-keyboard-focus policy; cards remain top-right, at most 360 logical pixels wide, with a scrollable stack inside the assigned space. This uses the existing layer-shell contract instead of adding a second output-discovery/scale/hotplug model to Notify or its vendored adapter.

Natural content measurement now controls the pointer input region, not the surface size. Startup and empty content are click-through; transparent space outside the visible stack stays click-through. Deferred input-region commands resolve the latest region at execution so they cannot restore an older region after replacement or clear. The trade-off is a rendering surface sized to the available output even when content is small or empty, rather than content-sized buffers. This supersedes the measured-surface resizing strategy, not natural card layout, ADR 0044's per-card timeout acknowledgement, or banner eligibility. Compositor presentation feedback, occlusion, oversized individual cards, long labels and full keyboard/accessibility acceptance remain separate concerns.

The [layer-shell protocol](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/swaywm/wlr-protocols/master/unstable/wlr-layer-shell-unstable-v1.xml) assigns omitted dimensions when opposite edges are anchored and supports empty input regions independently of keyboard interactivity. Notify keeps 12 logical pixels of margin on each edge and exclusive zone zero so the compositor can accommodate reserved desktop space.
