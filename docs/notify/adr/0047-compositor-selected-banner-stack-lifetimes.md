# Compositor-selected banner stack lifetimes

A permanently mapped startup surface pins every later banner to the startup output, and losing that output can terminate the renderer. Keep the renderer connection alive independently of its windows: use an empty startup probe for readiness, then create a compositor-selected surface for each new nonempty stack and retire it after the stack clears. A visible stack keeps its surface across replacements and focus changes so controls do not jump during interaction; the compositor chooses the focused or default output without a second Notify-owned output model.

If the compositor closes a displayed surface, recreate the still-current stack once on the compositor-selected fallback output. A fallback surface that closes before its first redraw, or any new surface that does not redraw within ten seconds, stops rendering under ADR 0043's explicit-restart policy rather than entering an unbounded reopen loop. This does not automatically restart a disconnected renderer or promise recovery after losing every output. Runtime identities, banner eligibility, revision-bound callbacks and already-started timeout deadlines are unchanged; hidden windows must not acknowledge newly queued content. Reserved space and geometry remain compositor-owned under ADRs 0045/0046.
