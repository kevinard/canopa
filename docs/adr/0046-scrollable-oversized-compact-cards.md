# Scrollable oversized compact cards

A natural card taller than the compositor viewport can never satisfy ADR 0044's full-card display condition, leaving finite notifications active indefinitely. Bound each compact card to that viewport and scroll its intact contents internally; acknowledge display when the entire bounded card viewport is visible in the surrounding stack. This extends ADRs 0044/0045 for oversized cards without changing banner eligibility, captured revision ownership, the two named-action projection or keyboard focus.

The sender's normal timeout starts at that first display, not after every line has been read; scrolling neither pauses nor resets it. Controls below the fold require scrolling, while cards clipped by the surrounding stack still wait for full visibility. Body previews remain independently bounded, and stack navigation must remain available alongside internal scrolling. This is not compositor presentation/occlusion feedback or certification of arbitrarily tiny outputs.

A single card may use the full viewport height. Multi-item stacks reserve 60 logical pixels (one ordinary Iced wheel step) of vertical space around each card, with a half-viewport minimum card budget on tiny outputs; otherwise a full-height middle card would require pixel-perfect positioning to acknowledge. Cards shorter than this budget retain their natural height. A separate 12px right gutter keeps the stack scrollbar clear of internal card scrollbars. Large wheel jumps can still skip cards; only a frame showing the full bounded card qualifies, and outputs below 120 logical pixels of available height are not covered by the ordinary-wheel reachability guarantee.
