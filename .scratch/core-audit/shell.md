# Shell core-experience audit

Status: needs-triage

Scope: Canopa Shell bars, Quick settings, built-in providers (`canopa-wifi-provider`,
`canopa-audio-provider`, `canopa-media-provider`, `canopa-shell --hardware-panel
bluetooth|power`), session packaging and the embedded default layout. The launcher
(`crates/canopa-shell/src/launcher/`) is out of scope. Read-only code review at
`20e1bf5` (2026-11-11). Nothing was built or run. Paths are relative to
`crates/canopa-shell/` unless they start with `packaging/`, `docs/` or `crates/`.

## Summary

- The architecture holds up. Status sources are event-driven over D-Bus, niri IPC or libpulse, and they degrade to "unavailable" and recover when a service disappears. Live reload works. Outputs reconcile from Wayland snapshots. The Bluetooth agent, Wi-Fi credential handling and MPRIS selection are careful work.
- The biggest daily gaps are things a user notices within an hour. There is no on-screen display (OSD) for anything, and brightness and play/pause keys are unbound. The shipped bar shows no battery, network, volume or tray. There is no Suspend. Wi-Fi can only *connect*, with no off, disconnect or forget.
- Robustness has one sharp edge. An invalid `config.kdl` at login makes `canopa-shell` exit, leaving no bar and no launcher, and systemd restarts it in a loop. A rejected live reload only writes a log line.
- Reboot and Power off run `systemctl` as a "bounded command": it is killed after 3 s, its result is ignored and the user gets no feedback.
- Missing outright: keyboard-layout indicator, night light, keyboard backlight, low-battery warning, translations (every string is hard-coded English, including the calendar's weekday names).
- Accessibility is narrower than the PRD implies. AT-SPI covers only provider panel content. The bar, Quick settings cards, menus (including the power confirmations), the calendar and tray menus expose nothing.
- Idle cost is mostly low. The one permanent wakeup source is the 250 ms config-metadata poll (about 4 wakeups/s for the whole session).

### PRD cross-check (W3, W5, W6)

- **W3, brightness and media keys:** confirmed, with one correction. Volume and mic keys are already bound (through `wpctl`, not Canopa). Brightness, play/pause and keyboard-backlight keys are unbound, and no OSD exists. `SetBrightness` through logind is already implemented (`hardware_panel/power.rs:83`).
- **W3, night light:** confirmed missing.
- **W3, network:** "Shell quick settings keep their current role" understates the gap. That role currently has no Wi-Fi on/off, disconnect, forget or wired/VPN awareness.
- **W3, Bluetooth:** confirmed. The Quick settings panel also lacks Forget and device battery, not only the Settings page.
- **W5:** confirmed. No i18n framework. Clock and calendar ignore the locale, and the calendar hard-codes English weekdays and a Monday start. Additional point: translatable labels also live in the embedded `default.kdl`.
- **W6:** partly refuted. "Generalize the existing AccessKit integration (Shell…)" suggests the Shell is covered. Only provider panel content is. The bar, Quick settings, menus, calendar and tray menus need the same work as Files and Settings.

## Ranked gaps

1. **No OSD, and brightness and media keys unbound.** *Impact:* every laptop user presses these keys on day one. Brightness keys do nothing, and volume keys work invisibly. *Approach:* add a passive overlay-layer OSD surface with its own lifecycle, separate from the Foreground (as the 2026-09-23 readiness audit recommended). Add CLI and IPC verbs (`canopa-shell volume up|down|mute`, `brightness up|down`, `media play-pause|next|previous`). They reuse `audio::adjust_default_sink_volume`, the logind `SetBrightness` path and the existing media `Apply`, and show the OSD. Bind the XF86 keys to them in `packaging/niri/defaults.kdl` with `allow-when-locked`. Keyboard backlight goes through logind `SetBrightness("leds", "*::kbd_backlight")`. *Size:* M.
2. **The default bar shows no battery, network, volume or tray.** *Impact:* laptop users can't see charge or connectivity at a glance, and tray-resident apps have no icon at all. *Approach:* add a compact status cluster to the `end` zone of `config/default.kdl` (battery when present, network, volume) plus a `tray` module. The existing modules already hide when unavailable. Fix the "Wi-Fi" title on wired networks by reading the primary connection type from NM (`PrimaryConnectionType`). *Size:* S (layout), S (network type).
3. **Invalid configuration takes the whole shell down at login, and rejected reloads are silent.** *Impact:* one typo in `config.kdl` leaves a user without bar or launcher and stuck in a restart loop. *Approach:* on a startup `ConfigError`, fall back to `Request::Defaults` (as `canopa-lock` already does, `crates/canopa-session/src/bin/lock.rs:125-133`). Show the safe diagnostic as a persistent bar badge or a Notify notification, and do the same when a reload is rejected (`configuration.last_error`). Add `StartLimitBurst` handling to the unit. *Size:* S–M.
4. **Session actions are incomplete and unreliable.** *Impact:* there is no Suspend (the most common laptop action). Reboot and Power off can silently do nothing when inhibited or when polkit asks for authentication. *Approach:* call logind directly over zbus: `Suspend`, `Hibernate` (only if `CanHibernate` answers `yes`), `Reboot`, `PowerOff`, with `interactive=true`. Do not use bounded `systemctl` commands. Use `Can*` to hide unsupported actions, `ListInhibitors` to show blockers in the confirmation, and surface errors in the menu. Log out should use `niri-ipc` `Action::Quit` (PRD W1). *Size:* M.
5. **Wi-Fi quick controls can only connect.** *Impact:* the user can't turn Wi-Fi off, disconnect, forget a bad network or see wired/VPN state without a terminal. *Approach:* in the existing NM and iwd backends, add `WirelessEnabled` (NM) and `Station`/`Device.Powered` (iwd) for the radio toggle; `Device.Disconnect`; `Settings.Connection.Delete` and `KnownNetwork.Forget` for forget. Add a VPN section that lists and activates existing NM VPN connections (creation stays in Settings, W3). *Size:* M.
6. **No keyboard-layout indicator.** *Impact:* multi-layout users (much of the non-US world) cannot see the active layout. *Approach:* a new module fed by niri's `KeyboardLayoutsChanged/Switched` events (already received, `app/niri.rs:183`). Click or scroll sends `Action::SwitchLayout`. *Size:* S.
7. **Accessibility of the bar, Quick settings and menus.** *Impact:* an Orca user can't operate the bar, toggle DND or confirm power actions. Keyboard users have no bind to reach Quick settings. *Approach:* lift `canopa_accessibility` into `canopa-ui` (PRD W6) and generate nodes for bar modules, Quick settings cards, menus and the calendar. Ship default binds, for example `Mod+A` for `--toggle-panel` and an IPC verb to focus the bar. *Size:* L.
8. **Night light.** *Impact:* an expected toggle is missing. *Approach:* first confirm that the Canopa compositor exposes `wlr-gamma-control-unstable-v1`. Then either ship `wl-gammarelay-rs` (Rust, D-Bus-controllable) and drive it from a Quick settings toggle with a sunrise/sunset schedule (PRD principle: existing Rust tool), or implement gamma-control in a small Canopa session helper. *Size:* M.
9. **Translations.** *Impact:* the desktop is English-only, and the calendar is wrong for US and Middle-East week starts. *Approach:* PRD W5, using Fluent with `i18n-embed`. Give default KDL labels message keys (for example `label-id "session-reboot"`), or synthesise labels for built-in modules. Use ICU4X for date and week data. *Size:* L.
10. **Quick settings cards are shallow.** *Impact:* volume needs repeated "+" presses, brightness needs a drill-in, and Bluetooth and Power buttons are static and appear without hardware. *Approach:* inline sliders (protocol sliders already exist, ADR 0012) for volume and brightness, plus a mute toggle. Show the Bluetooth adapter state and connected-device count. Hide the Bluetooth, Power and brightness cards when BlueZ has no adapter, or there is no backlight and no PPD. *Size:* M.
11. **No low-battery warning or time remaining.** *Impact:* surprise shutdowns. *Approach:* read UPower `WarningLevel`, `TimeToEmpty` and `TimeToFull` in `canopa-widgets::battery`, send a Notify notification at Low/Critical, and show the time in the tooltip and card. *Size:* S.
12. **Bluetooth device management.** *Impact:* the user can't remove a stale pairing or see headphone battery. *Approach:* `Adapter1.RemoveDevice`, `Battery1.Percentage`, `Device1.Icon`. Consider replacing the 2 s poll with `ObjectManager` signals. *Size:* S–M.
13. **Provider failure UX.** *Impact:* jargon errors with no way to recover. *Approach:* a plain-language failure with a Retry action, rendered through the Canopa document so it is accessible. Delay the "unavailable" text in the hardware providers until the first read completes. *Size:* S.
14. **MPRIS depth.** *Impact:* no artwork or scrubbing. *Approach:* `mpris:artUrl` through the existing icon/image path, plus `Position`/`Seeked` and a `SetPosition` slider. *Size:* S–M.
15. **Idle wakeups.** *Impact:* a small battery cost all day. *Approach:* replace the 250 ms poll with inotify on the parent directories (the `notify` crate, keeping the re-resolve semantics of ADR 0021), or back it off to 1–2 s. Use exponential backoff for the Notify reconnect. *Size:* S.
16. **Privacy indicators.** *Impact:* no signal that the mic or a screencast is in use. *Approach:* PipeWire stream observation (source-output with an active link) and portal ScreenCast session state, shown as a bar badge. *Size:* M.

## Bugs or risks found while reading

- **Startup exits on any config error.** `main.rs:95-108` exits 1 when `try_main` fails at `config::load` (`main.rs:328`). Syntax and envelope errors in the shared file affect every consumer (`docs/configuration.md:53`). The result is a 3 s restart loop with no shell.
- **Power actions are killed after 3 s.** Menu `command` items use `CommandPolicy` with a default of 3000 ms (`config/module.rs:32`) and `spawn_command_with_timeout_fire_and_forget` (`command.rs:223-247`). If `systemctl reboot` waits for polkit authentication (for example when other sessions are logged in), it is SIGKILLed mid-prompt. The exit status is discarded (`app/app_effects.rs:183`).
- **Each bounded command gets its own busy-wait thread.** The thread polls `try_wait` every 10 ms (`command.rs:242`) for up to 3 s, about 300 wakeups per click. A `pidfd` or tokio child wait would avoid it.
- **`canopa-shell lock` still execs swaylock** (`wallpaper/locker.rs:5-24`, `main.rs:82`), while the UI uses `canopa-lock`. Scripts or docs that call the CLI fail on a stock Canopa install without swaylock.
- **The volume keys can over-amplify.** The `wpctl set-volume … 5%+` binds have no `-l 1.0` limit (`packaging/niri/defaults.kdl:39`). Shell-side adjustment and the keys use different backends and caps.
- **The hardware providers flash a false "unavailable".** Both publish an empty snapshot before the first D-Bus read (`hardware_panel/bluetooth.rs:766`, `hardware_panel/power.rs:270`). The power provider also re-runs the logind session lookup every 3 s (`power.rs:208-211`).
- **The provider failure document is not accessible.** `surface/panel.rs:71-79` builds a v1 `Elements` document, which `sync_canopa_accessibility` skips (`app/canopa_accessibility.rs:574-581`), so screen-reader users get silence on failure.
- **The network card is labelled "Wi-Fi" unconditionally** (`modules/network.rs:243-247`), which is misleading on Ethernet or with no Wi-Fi hardware.
- **Stale documentation and defaults.** `README.md:33,146,177` say restart is required, contradicting ADR 0021. `config/new_default.kdl` is unused, but its comments claim `overlay` is the default layer (the code default is `Top`, `config/bar.rs:157`) and use an outdated `collapsed-width` layout. It also still ships `command`-style launch examples. Remove it or regenerate it from `default.kdl`.
- **Niri defaults hard-code Catppuccin colours** (`packaging/niri/defaults.kdl:7`), so the focus ring ignores the Canopa accent and light scheme.
- **Logout depends on `niri` from PATH** (`config/default.kdl:33`) while the session runs `canopa-compositor`. This works only while the IPC stays wire-compatible (PRD W1).
- **The tray watcher is tied to a configured tray module** (`app/subscriptions.rs:50-75`). With the default layout, nothing owns `org.kde.StatusNotifierWatcher`. Some apps (Electron) then fall back to no tray or refuse to minimise.

## Hands-on checks needed

1. **Idle wakeups.** Leave the desktop idle with no panel open. Run `pidstat -w -t -p $(pidof canopa-shell) 10 6` and `sudo powertop` (Overview, canopa-shell row). Repeat with `canopa-notify` stopped. Expected: about 4/s from the config poll plus 1/s from the Notify retry. Record the baseline.
2. **Invalid config at login.** Add `shell { bar "x" { edge "nowhere" } }` (or a stray `{`) to `~/.config/canopa/config.kdl`. Run `systemctl --user restart canopa-shell` and watch `journalctl --user -u canopa-shell -f`. Confirm there is no bar, a restart loop, and that Mod+Space does nothing. Then fix the file live and see whether the shell comes back on its own.
3. **Rejected reload feedback.** With the shell running, introduce an invalid option and save. Confirm the old layout stays and that nothing visible tells the user.
4. **Reboot under polkit or inhibitors.** (a) Run `systemd-inhibit --what=shutdown --mode=block sleep 600 &`, then Quick settings, Reboot, Reboot. Note the feedback. (b) Log in a second user on a TTY and repeat. Does a canopa-polkit prompt appear, and does it vanish after about 3 s?
5. **Suspend path.** Confirm there is no UI route other than `systemctl suspend` from a terminal. Check that lock-before-sleep still works through canopa-lock and Stasis.
6. **Monitors.** With a laptop and an external monitor at a different scale: hotplug with Quick settings open; suspend and resume while docked; change scale with `niri msg output … scale`. Check bar size, panel output and workspace filtering.
7. **Tray.** Add `tray` to the bar end zone in Settings. Run `nm-applet --indicator`, Steam, Discord or Element, KeePassXC and Telegram. Check left click on menu-only items, then `systemctl --user restart canopa-shell` and see whether each item re-registers.
8. **Bluetooth.** Run `rfkill block bluetooth`, open the panel and press Turn on. Pair headphones (no-input agent) and a phone (passkey confirm). Close the panel mid-pairing. Check whether a paired device can be removed.
9. **Brightness.** On a laptop, move the slider and check `/sys/class/backlight/*/brightness`. On a desktop with an external monitor, note the message and whether the card is still shown. Press the XF86 brightness keys (expect nothing).
10. **Volume keys.** Press raise repeatedly and confirm there is no OSD and that the level can exceed 100%. Compare with scroll on a bar volume module.
11. **Provider crash.** Open Wi-Fi, run `pkill -f canopa-wifi-provider`, and read the message. Check whether reopening recovers it.
12. **Missing services.** One at a time, `sudo systemctl stop NetworkManager`, then `bluetooth`, `upower` and `power-profiles-daemon`. Watch each card and bar module, then start the service again and confirm recovery without a shell restart.
13. **Screen reader.** Start Orca. Tab through the bar (expect nothing announced), Quick settings, the Wi-Fi provider, the Log out confirmation and the calendar. Record what is spoken.
14. **Keyboard only.** Unplug the mouse. Try to toggle DND, open Wi-Fi and reboot using only the keyboard and the default binds.
15. **Night-light feasibility.** Run `wl-gammarelay-rs` or `gammastep -O 3500` under `canopa-compositor` to confirm gamma-control support before choosing an approach.
